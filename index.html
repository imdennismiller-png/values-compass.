Shopping sensitivity

Alternatives explorer

Research candidates are not endorsements. A company with unknown evidence is never treated as neutral. Retail, software and banking are different categories and should not be compared as direct substitutes.

Five distinct statuses

Supporting evidence Included evidence supports the selected position.

Conflicting evidence Included evidence conflicts with the selected position.

Mixed Both supporting and opposing actions, shown separately even when they cancel numerically.

Neutral Research-supported non-advocacy; not the same as no evidence.

Unknown No adequate evidence; excluded from the score and counted as missing coverage.

Illustrative scoring

For an issue, positive and negative findings are accumulated separately. Each finding's strength is weighted by your issue importance and conviction. The net impact is capped at ±100 per issue; Mixed remains visible regardless of net impact. Neutral has zero impact. Unknown has no score and lowers coverage. Overall alignment is shown only when at least half of weighted issue importance is covered; otherwise it is unrated.

Automatic findings have equal illustrative strength. Source notes identify the date, type, and scope of each action. Your reviewed finding takes precedence when it uses the same source for that company and issue. Scores are provisional, and overall alignment requires at least 50% weighted evidence coverage.
